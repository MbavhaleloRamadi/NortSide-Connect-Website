Origin story

Born in the 2024 A.I. revolution

NortSide Connect emerged with a singular vision: to democratise advanced technology for small and medium businesses in South Africa.

Having seen firsthand how the right tech transforms a struggling business into a market leader, our founder set out to create solutions that aren't just innovative — they're immediately valuable and practical for real-world challenges.

Today, we serve clients across multiple industries with integrated solutions ranging from AI agent development to CIPC company registration — everything a growing business needs under one roof.
